Classical Textual Interpretations (Brihat Samhita & Surya Siddhanta)

In Sanskrit astronomical literature, Rohini Shakata Bheda (piercing of the wain/cart of Rohini) is regarded as a major celestial phenomenon accompanied by classical omen descriptions. Below are the authentic primary text citations and planetary interpretations:

1. Saturn (शनि शकट भेदन)

"रोहिणीशकटमर्कजौ भिन्दन लोके करोति महदशुभम्।
प्रजापतिपीडया जगदशेषमुपतापमेति सुभृशम्॥" — वृहत्संहिता ४७.३

Translation & Meaning: When Saturn pierces the cart of Rohini, severe affliction strikes the world. The entire population suffers from extreme distress, famine, and destruction. (Brihat Samhita 47.3)

2. Mars (मंगल शकट भेदन)

"रुधिरो रोहिणीशकटं भित्त्वा याति यदा तदा महद्भयम्।
शस्त्रानलाग्निपीडा च प्रजानां जायते महती॥" — वृहत्संहिता ४७.४

Translation & Meaning: When Mars pierces Rohini Shakata, great fear, war, bloodshed, and fire hazards afflict the subjects. (Brihat Samhita 47.4)

3. Moon & Venus (चंद्र एवं शुक्र शकट भेदन)

When the Moon or Venus pierces the cart of Rohini, the text notes acute meteorological and agricultural consequences, severe drought, and disruption of trade and sea routes.

4. Sun, Jupiter & Mercury (सूर्य, गुरु एवं बुध शकट भेदन)

Brihat Samhita identifies that when benevolent bodies pass through the cart without extreme southern latitude, the affliction is mitigated, whereas direct piercing by any malefic body produces severe geopolitical unrest.

Scientific Methodological Disclaimer: Classical Sanskrit texts assign omens (फलित) to rare astronomical alignments. These historical predictions represent cultural and observational records of ancient India. They do not constitute modern physical causal laws connecting planetary positions to weather or human affairs.

Sources & Methodological Documentation

A. Surya Siddhanta (8.13)

"वृषे सप्तदशे भागे यस्य याम्योऽंशकद्वयात् ।
विक्षेपोऽभ्यधिको भिन्द्याद् रोहिण्याः शकटं तु सः ॥"

Geometric Rule Definition: When any planet is situated in the 17th degree of Taurus (Nirayana / Sidereal 16°00'00" to 17°00'00" Taurus, or 46°..47° ecliptic longitude) and its southern ecliptic latitude exceeds 2° (Latitude < -2.0°), it pierces the cart of Rohini.

B. Brihat Samhita (Varahamihira)

Defines Shakata Bheda as the geometric passage of a planet through the V-shaped cart formed by the Hyades star cluster (α, θ1, θ2, γ, δ, ε Tauri) or direct collision/occultation with Aldebaran (Rohini junction star at Nirayana Longitude ~49°40', Latitude -5°28').

C. Published Research Method (Sarma / Iyengar / Vahia et al.)

Reproduces the cartesian-ecliptic boundary method relative to the epoch-adjusted coordinates of Aldebaran and the Hyades cluster, establishing a bounded polygon [Ecliptic Longitude 45.5° to 50.5°, Ecliptic Latitude -5.5° to -2.5°].

Why Taurus Transit is NOT Shakata Bheda

A common misconception in popular astrology equates every routine planetary transit through Taurus (वृषभ राशि) or Rohini Nakshatra with Rohini Shakata Bheda. In rigorous Sanskrit astronomy, this is mathematically false:

  • 1. Taurus Transit (वृषभ गोचर): Refers to a body having ecliptic longitude between 30° and 60°. Saturn enters Taurus approximately every 29.5 years and remains there for ~2.5 years.
  • 2. Rohini Nakshatra Entry (रोहिणी प्रवेश): Refers to longitude between 40°00'00" and 53°20'00" Sidereal. Happens frequently for all planets.
  • 3. Rohini Shakata Bheda (रोहिणी शकट भेदन): Requires a simultaneous specific longitude AND extreme southern latitude condition (Latitude < -2° in 17th degree of Taurus, or passage through the Hyades V-cart).

Because Saturn's orbital inclination to the ecliptic is 2.485°, Saturn's southern latitude exceeds 2° only near its descending node when aligned with specific orbital geometry. Consequently, Saturn Shakata Bheda is a very rare event occurring only a few times per millennium, not every 30 years!
